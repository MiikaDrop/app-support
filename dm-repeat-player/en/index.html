DM Repeat Player
Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026
Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Introduction

mikkabo (the “Developer”) establishes this Privacy Policy to explain how user information is handled in the Android application DM Repeat Player (the “App”).

2. Access to audio files on the device

The App accesses audio files stored on the user’s device and related metadata, such as title, artist, album, file name, storage location, and duration, in order to find, list, and play those files.

This access is used solely to provide the App’s music playback features. The App does not modify or delete audio files.

3. Information stored on the device

The App stores the following information locally on the user’s device:

  • Playlists created by the user and information about the tracks added to them
  • Playback state, including playback position and playback mode
  • Settings such as display language, theme, and colors
  • Settings required for App features, such as the sleep timer

This information is used to provide the App’s features and restore its state. Depending on the Android backup settings, the device operating system or a backup service selected by the user may back up this information.

4. External transmission, collection, and sharing

The App does not transmit audio files, audio metadata, playlists, settings, or other user information to the Developer’s servers or to third-party servers.

The App does not collect user information or share it with third parties.

5. Advertising, analytics, and accounts

The App does not use advertising, analytics, behavioral tracking, or advertising identifiers. It does not require users to create an account or sign in.

6. Permissions

The App uses Android permissions for the following purposes:

  • Audio access: to find, display, and play music stored on the device
  • Notifications: to display playback status and provide playback controls during background playback
  • Foreground service: to continue music playback in the background and while the screen is off

Users can change granted permissions in Android settings.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not designed for children and does not collect personal information, including age.

8. Data retention and deletion

Playlists, playback state, and settings stored locally by the App are retained while the App is in use or until the user deletes them. Users can delete playlists within the App. Users can delete all information stored by the App by clearing the App’s data in Android settings or uninstalling the App.

The App does not delete audio files stored on the device.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in response to changes in the App, laws, or regulations. Important changes will be announced on this page or on the App’s distribution page.
